# Hypotheses Register

A holding pen. Ideas for Papers arrive faster than the Canon can support them; they wait here until the concepts they depend on have been derived in dialogue. No hypothesis leaves this register for the Volume until every concept it uses exists in the Canon.

An entry records: the candidate distinction, the question, the concepts it depends on, and its current obstacle.

---

## H-01 — Representation ≠ Reality *(absorbs the earlier Reality ≠ Data)*

**Question:** can an organisation be understood through the tools it uses?
**Thesis sketch:** software represents organisational activity; it is not the organisation itself. Data is a species of representation: it records events, while reality includes everything that gives those events meaning.
**Depends on:** Representation (pending) · Reality (pending, possibly primitive).
**Note:** originally two hypotheses (Representation ≠ Reality; Reality ≠ Data); collapsed — one distinction, one Paper.

## H-02 — The Observer's method *(absorbs Seeing ≠ Looking · Observation ≠ Measurement · Interpretation ≠ Opinion)*

**Question:** why do organisations remain invisible to those inside them?
**Thesis sketches:** looking gathers information, seeing reveals structure; measurement records variables, observation reveals relationships; interpretation discovers meaning already present, opinion projects meaning onto reality.
**Depends on:** Observation (provisional) · Interpretation (pending) · Understanding (pending).
**Note:** three facets of one method. May become one Paper, or feed Papers 007 and 011 directly. Not three Papers.

## H-03 — Visibility ≠ Legibility

**Question:** what makes an organisation understandable?
**Thesis sketch:** an organisation can be perfectly visible while remaining impossible to read.
**Depends on:** Legibility (pending).
**Genealogy to confront:** James C. Scott, *Seeing Like a State* — the major precedent for legibility applied to organisations and states; the Paper must cite it and go beyond it.

## H-04 — Context ≠ Background

**Question:** what makes the same decision right in one organisation and wrong in another?
**Thesis sketch:** context is not where action happens; context determines what action means.
**Depends on:** Context (already pending in the Glossary — convergence noted).

## H-05 — Form ≠ Structure

**Question:** when everything changes, what remains?
**Thesis sketch:** forms evolve; structures persist; architecture exists to preserve structure while allowing form to change.
**Depends on:** Structure (primitive) · Pattern (Dialogue 004) · the invariant (Dialogue 002).
**Note:** this Paper would be the public articulation of the Canon's primitive — the primitive is not derived internally, but may be revealed publicly. Its thesis restates the invariant of Dialogue 002: the strongest early candidate in this register.

## H-07 — Principle ≠ Implementation

**Question:** what is the difference between what an organisation is committed to and how that commitment is built?
**Thesis sketch:** the pattern is the principle; the architecture is the implementation of the principle; the form is its concrete manifestation. Architecture belongs to the implementation side. A pattern is discovered; an implementation is chosen and replaced.
**Depends on:** Pattern (Dialogue 004) · Architecture (provisional) · the Ladder of Realisation.
**Note:** supersedes the earlier "Pattern ≠ Architecture", which mis-posed the pair as opposites when they are adjacent levels. Completes the triptych with H-05: **form changes · pattern persists · architecture preserves.** Carries the discipline's cleanest positioning result: an implementation is not an architecture. A specific product (e.g. DECIMA) is an architectural implementation, one level below architecture — same pattern, same architecture, different form across decades.

## H-06 — Architecture ≠ Intervention

**Question:** must every observation produce action?
**Thesis sketch:** architecture begins by understanding what already exists, not by changing it.
**Depends on:** Architecture (provisional) · Observation (provisional).
**Note:** grounds the method (Observe before Architect) and the candidate axiom on intelligibility.

## Absorbed / resolved elsewhere

- **Seeing ≠ Looking · Observation ≠ Measurement · Interpretation ≠ Opinion** → absorbed into H-02.
- **Presence ≠ Control** → not a separate Paper. Paper 011 (*Observation ≠ Control*) already carries presence in its thesis ("the observer replaces inspection with presence"). Whether *presence* is the deeper term is a question for the dialogue on Observation, not for a duplicate Paper.

## Positioning note (for 003-boundaries or 004-method, when mature)

The organisational architect does not modernise an organisation. He reveals the structure that already exists — and only then redesigns it. The discipline's posture is revelation before intervention. *(Genealogy: Sellars does not update Mozart; he reveals that Mozart remains current. Michelangelo: the statue already exists in the marble.)*
