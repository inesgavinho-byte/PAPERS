# Glossary

Every word the discipline depends on. A term is either **closed** (derived in a dialogue, entered in the Canon), **provisional** (a working definition carried from dialogue, not yet formally derived), or **pending** (listed, not yet defined).

The Glossary never introduces a term the Canon has not authorised or a dialogue has not examined.

---

## Closed

**Organisation** — a living system that coordinates people, knowledge and resources towards a shared purpose across time. Its identity is preserved by its constitutive pattern; its architecture creates the conditions under which that pattern can be preserved; its continuity depends on the system's capacity to decide and to learn. *(Canon entry; derived in Dialogue 001, corrected in Dialogue 004.)*

**System** — a structure that preserves a pattern despite change, independently of the particular agents involved. *(Canon entry; derived in Dialogue 002, simplified in Dialogue 004.)*

**Pattern** — what stays the same when the particulars change. *(Canon entry; derived in Dialogue 004. The discipline's operational primitive. Invariant is its technical name, seen from the point of view of preservation.)*

**Living system** — a system that preserves its constitutive pattern. *(Canon entry; derived in Dialogue 003, simplified once Pattern closed. Its continuity is a consequence, not the definition. Genealogy: Maturana's autopoiesis.)*

## Provisional

**Structure** — parts organised in relation. *Primitive term. Not derived.* (*Organised* does not imply intention: a crystal, a snowflake, an ant colony are organised. A heap of sand is not.)

**Invariant** — the technical name for what a system preserves: what remains true despite change. Used in the discipline as an operator of inquiry (see Method): of anything examined, ask *what is the invariant?* *(The publication's purpose — Thinking for what remains — named this concept before the Canon did.)*

**Architecture** — the structure through which a system observes, remembers, decides and learns; it creates the conditions under which a pattern can be preserved. *(Defined over systems, not organisations, to avoid circularity. Architecture does not itself preserve — the system does; architecture makes preservation possible, as a bridge's structure makes bearing loads possible without itself bearing them. Deliberateness is not part of the definition: most organisations carry accidental architecture. The discipline exists to replace accidental architecture with deliberate architecture.)*

**Observation** — the systematic capture of reality as it is, prior to interpretation, judgement or intervention.

**Judgement** — the human act of deciding under irreducible uncertainty, where values carry weight. Decisions can be produced by machines; judgement cannot.

**Evolution** — the continuous adaptation of an organisation through changes to its architecture informed by observed reality. *(Links the triad: Observation → Architecture → Evolution.)*

**Enterprise** — an organisation that sustains its purpose through the creation of economic value.

**Company** — a legal form through which an enterprise operates. *(The discipline does not depend on legal concepts; the object of the science is the organisation. A company is neither the object nor the boundary of the discipline.)*
