# Axioms

The truths that do not change. An axiom is admitted only when no dialogue has been able to produce a counterexample, and it is retired the day one appears.

All entries below are **candidates** until stressed in dialogue.

---

1. **Architecture follows observation.** No structure may be designed for a reality that has not been read.

2. **Knowledge precedes judgement.** Judgement exercised without knowledge is not judgement; it is preference.

3. **Systems outlive people.** Grounded in the Canon: the identity of an organisation is preserved by its architecture, not by its members.

4. **Every organisation is already an architecture.** Most architecture is accidental — inherited, sedimented, never designed. The discipline replaces accidental architecture with deliberate architecture.

5. **The object of the science is the organisation; the initial market is the enterprise.** The discipline must never become a prisoner of the product.

6. **Observation is continuous, not episodic.** What is observed continuously does not need to be audited.

7. **A definition that closes inquiry is defective.** Good definitions open questions.

8. **The first responsibility of architecture is not to change reality, but to make it intelligible.** This axiom does not merely accompany Axiom 1; it grounds it. Axiom 1 states the order — architecture follows observation. This one states why the order exists: nothing may be redesigned before it has been made legible. *(Posture of the discipline: revelation before intervention.)*

## Structural Laws (reserved — not yet promoted)

An axiom constrains the discipline's own conduct. A **structural law** is different: it is an observed regularity that explains a whole family of phenomena, across domains, without defining anything. Laws do not belong in the Glossary or on either Ladder. This block is reserved for them.

Nothing is promoted here yet: a regularity needs many instances before it may be declared a law; three examples are a conjecture, not a law. Two seeds are recorded, pending instances:

- *Every enduring creation separates principle from implementation, and implementation from manifestation.* (Instances so far: music, architecture, DECIMA, the Canon itself. Seed only.)
- *Patterns endure by changing form, never by preserving form.* (Seed only.)
