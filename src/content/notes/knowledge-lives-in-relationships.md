title: "Knowledge lives in relationships."
published: "2026-06-25"
excerpt: "The org chart shows reporting lines. It hides the only lines that matter."
concepts:

  - Memory
---

The org chart shows who reports to whom. It says nothing about who actually knows what, or who would call whom at 7pm when something breaks.

That second graph — invisible, undocumented, entirely relational — is where the organisation's real knowledge lives. Lose a node in it and you lose not a person but a set of connections that no document was holding.

Make the relationships explicit before they leave the building.
