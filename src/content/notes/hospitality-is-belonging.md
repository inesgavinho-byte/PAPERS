Service makes you comfortable. Hospitality makes you feel you were expected.

The difference is not in the gesture but in the recognition behind it. Good service is a transaction performed well. Hospitality is the sense, however briefly, that a place was holding a space for you — that you belong here, at least for now.

You cannot script that. You can only design the conditions in which it becomes possible.
