A tool that removes the need to think slowly removes the ability to.

The promise of automation is to take work off our hands. The risk is that it takes understanding with it. The useful question is not *what can this system decide for us* but *what judgement does it let us exercise that we could not before*.

Amplify judgement. Keep the responsibility human. Anything that quietly relocates accountability into a model is not assistance — it is abdication.
